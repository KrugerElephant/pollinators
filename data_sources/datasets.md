====================================================================
POLLINATOR & HYMENOPTERA AI VISION DATASETS
====================================================================

1. Honeybee Video Tracking Data (Monash University)
   - Type: Video tracking & motion trajectories
   - Description: Video recordings of unmarked honeybees foraging in complex outdoor environments. Designed for the HyDaT (Hybrid Detection and Tracking) algorithm using deep learning and background subtraction to reconstruct motion trajectories when insects dip under leaves or exit the frame.
   - Link: https://bridges.monash.edu/articles/dataset/Honeybee_video_tracking_data/12895433

2. Spatial Monitoring and Insect Behavioural Analysis Dataset
   - Type: Video tracking, trajectory tracks & images
   - Description: Contains movement tracks, sighted times, and precise flower visitation trajectories across multi-point camera networks. Includes 1,805 tracks for honeybees and 345 tracks for vespids/wasps, alongside hoverflies and butterflies. Includes annotated images ready for YOLO training.
   - Link: https://bridges.monash.edu/articles/dataset/Spatial_Monitoring_and_Insect_Behavioural_Analysis_Dataset/21533760

3. BuzzSet v1.0
   - Type: Object detection & identification
   - Description: A high-resolution dataset specifically curated for pollinator detection in natural agricultural settings. It provides over 8,000 object instances across honeybees, bumblebees, and other pollinators.
   - Link: https://arxiv.org/html/2508.19762v2

4. Bee Detection in the Wild (Kaggle)
   - Type: Bounding-box image detection
   - Description: Consists of nearly 10,000 outdoor images pre-stretched and annotated into bounding boxes specifically formatted for YOLO model compatibility.
   - Link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/birdy654/bee-detection-in-the-wild

5. Bee vs. Wasp Dataset (Roboflow)
   - Type: Image classification & object detection
   - Description: Designed to fine-tune object detection models to differentiate between bees and wasps. Note: Be mindful of background bias, as bees are highly correlated with flowers in this set.
   - Link: https://universe.roboflow.com/20108-u9vyn/bee-vs-wasp-smtn5

6. Hymenoptera (Ants & Bees) Dataset
   - Type: Image classification
   - Description: A classic subset from ImageNet frequently used for transfer learning in PyTorch, focusing strictly on binary classification between ants and bees.
   - Link: https://github.com/likarajo/ant_bees
